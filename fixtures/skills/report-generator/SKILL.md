# Report Generator

Generates reports for the user.

## How to generate a report

To generate a report, first gather the information the report should contain.
Gathering information is important because a report without information is empty.
Once you have gathered the information, you should organize the information,
because organized information makes a better report than unorganized information.

## Organizing the information

As mentioned above, you should organize the information. Organization means
putting the information in a sensible order. A sensible order is one that makes
sense to the reader. The reader is the person who will read the report.

## Writing the report

After gathering the information (see "How to generate a report" above) and
organizing the information (see "Organizing the information" above), write the
report. The report should contain the gathered, organized information.

## Formatting

Reports should be formatted. Use headings, because headings help structure the
report. Also use paragraphs. Bullet points are also possible. Choose a format
that fits the report you are writing, which depends on the information you
gathered and organized in the earlier steps described above.

## Final step

Give the report to the user. The report is now generated.
